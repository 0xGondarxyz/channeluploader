# Channel Uploader

Channel Uploader is a private tool. It uploads videos that its owner made to the owner's own YouTube channels. It has no other users.

# Privacy Policy

## YouTube API Services

Channel Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's handling of your data is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What the app accesses

- **Upload access** (`youtube.upload`): to upload videos and set their thumbnails on your channel.
- **Read-only channel info** (`youtube.readonly`): to read your channel's ID and name, so each video goes to the right channel.

The app does not read, change or delete any other YouTube data.

## How data is stored

- The sign-in token for each channel is stored only on the owner's own computer.
- No data is sent to any server other than Google's.
- No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for ads.
- The app collects no analytics and uses no cookies.

## How to remove access

You can remove the app's access at any time on your [Google Account permissions page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Deleting the stored token file on the computer also removes it.
